Archived Plans Index

Purpose
=======
Quick reference for completed implementation plans.
Each entry tells you why it existed, what shipped, and where to read more.

How to extend this index
========================
1) Add a new entry with these fields:
   - Plan: filename
   - Why: 1-2 sentences on the problem or motivation
   - Outcome: 2-4 bullets on what shipped
   - PR: link or number
2) Add new entries at the top (newest first).
